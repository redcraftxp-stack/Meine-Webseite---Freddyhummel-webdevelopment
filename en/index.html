Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Get in touch – I'll be happy to help.

How much does a website cost?

The price depends on the scope of the project, including the number of pages, features and the content already available. After a short conversation, you'll receive a quote before any work begins.

How long does development take?

Most projects take a few days, or up to about a week depending on their complexity. We'll agree on the exact timeline before starting.

What if I don't have any text or images yet?

That's absolutely fine. We'll go through the content together. I'll help organise it and explain exactly what materials are still needed.

Can you redesign my existing website?

In most cases, yes. Existing content can often be reused, and I'll try to keep your current page URLs wherever possible to help preserve your search engine visibility.

Can I update the content myself later?

If you'd like, I can set up your website so you can edit text and images yourself. Alternatively, I can make updates for you.

Will my website appear on Google?

I'll build a solid technical foundation with a clear structure, relevant page titles, fast loading times and local information for Donzdorf and the surrounding area. However, no one can honestly guarantee specific search rankings.

Do you work outside Donzdorf?

Yes. I work locally in the Donzdorf and Göppingen area, but projects can also be handled entirely online.

What happens after the website launches?

If you wish, I can take care of updates and small changes. You can also contact me if something isn't working as expected.
